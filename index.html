Cast and Crew

Pranav Mohanlal

Pranav Mohanlal is an Indian actor who primarily works in the Malayalam film industry.He won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Child Artist for his debut role in the 2002 film Punarjani.He returned to acting as a lead star with the 2018 action thriller Aadi and achieved blockbuster success with Hridayam in 2022.

Kalyani Priyadarshan

Kalyani Priyadarshan is an Indian actress who works primarily in Malayalam, Tamil, and Telugu films.She made her acting debut in the 2017 Telugu film Hello and won the Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut.She achieved massive commercial success by starring alongside Pranav Mohanlal in the blockbuster movie Hridayam.

Darshana Rajendran

Darshana Rajendran is an Indian actress and singer who primarily works in the Malayalam film industry. She made her film debut with John Paul Vaathil Thurakkunnu in 2014 and earned acclaim for her natural performances. She became widely popular through Hridayam and later starred in the successful film Jaya Jaya Jaya Jaya Hey.
